## Modified dependency

Fluxion includes a source patch to Apache-licensed librqbit for per-torrent connection filtering and magnet tracker handling. Download the <a href="/licenses/librqbit-fluxion-source.zip" download>patched librqbit source archive</a> with its license and modification notes. The [Fluxion repository](https://github.com/backrunner/fluxion) contains the wider project; development changes may precede their appearance there.
