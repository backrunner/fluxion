## Contact support

<SupportForm locale="en" />

The private OnFire intake is reserved for a later setup. Until it opens, public, non-sensitive bug reports can be filed in the [source issue tracker](https://github.com/backrunner/fluxion/issues). **Do not post private information there.** We do not currently provide a private privacy-request channel; that must be enabled before the app is submitted for release.

## Prepare a useful report

Include the app version, macOS version, transfer protocol, steps to reproduce, and exact error text. Say whether a proxy is enabled and whether the destination is local, removable or cloud-backed.

Do not include passwords, cookies, authorization headers, signed download URLs, private tracker addresses, private keys, personal file paths or confidential file contents. Review screenshots and logs before sharing them.

Apple handles purchases made through the Mac App Store. Once that edition is available, use [Apple purchase support](https://support.apple.com/billing) for billing questions.
